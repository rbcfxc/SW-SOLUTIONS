Refund, Cancellation and Cooling-off Policy

Last updated: 15 November 2026

1. The short version

  • Before I start work: you can cancel and get a full refund.
  • After work has started: I make the agreed revisions (3 rounds within 3 days of delivery) until what you ordered is delivered as agreed. If you cancel a started order, you get a refund of the part not yet done, and I may keep a fair amount for work already done.
  • Your legal rights are never reduced by this policy.

2. Cancelling before work starts

If you cancel before I begin work on your order, I refund 100% of what you paid. I will tell you in writing when work begins (for example “I have started your store”). I refund within 7 business days and no later than 30 days, to the same account or payment method you used.

3. Cancelling after work has started

Every store, website and logo is made to your selections and details. If you cancel after work has begun:

  1. I tell you in writing what has been done.
  2. I refund the part not yet done. I may keep a fair amount for work already completed, based on how far the work has gone, and I will explain the amount in writing. Third-party costs I have already paid for you (such as a domain, theme or app) are not refundable.
  3. If I fail to deliver what we agreed, for reasons within my control, you may cancel and I refund the part not delivered.
  4. If the work is materially defective and I have not fixed it after reasonable attempts, you are entitled to a fair refund for the defective part.

4. Cooling-off rights

ECTA (section 44). If you are an individual buying online, the Electronic Communications and Transactions Act 25 of 2002 generally lets you cancel without giving a reason within 7 days after the agreement for services is concluded.

Exceptions. The Act says this right does not apply to certain transactions, including goods or services made to your specifications or clearly personalised. Because I build every store, website and logo to your instructions and details, I treat my services as personalised.

My promise. If you cancel within 7 days and I have not yet started work, you get a full refund (section 2). If you asked me to start immediately and then cancel within the 7 days, section 3 applies. Where the law gives you a stronger right, the law applies.

CPA (section 16). The 5-business-day right to cancel applies to direct marketing, meaning where I approached you without you asking. It does not apply when you contact me or order from the website. If I ever approach you directly, I will honour it.

5. Store Maintenance subscription

  • Cancel at any time. Message me on WhatsApp or email info@metsing.store with “cancel maintenance” at least 5 days before your next billing date.
  • The service ends at the end of the month you have paid for. I will not invoice you again, and I will confirm in writing.
  • A month that has already started is not refunded, unless I failed to provide the service.
  • If I materially fail to provide the service, I will refund or credit the affected period.

6. Company Consultation

  • Before I submit anything to an authority: full refund of my fee.
  • After submission: my fee for work already done is not refundable, because it cannot be undone. I refund any part not yet done.
  • Government and statutory fees are paid to the authority and are governed by its rules. I cannot refund them. If I paid on your behalf and the authority refunds me, I pass it on.
  • If I make a mistake that costs you, I will fix it at my cost and refund my fee for that work where fair, without limiting your rights.

7. How to ask for a refund

Email info@metsing.store or WhatsApp +27 64 499 7135 with your name, order details and reason. I reply within 2 business days.

8. Faulty or misdescribed work

If the work does not match what was agreed or is not of reasonable quality, tell me within 7 days of handover. I will fix it at no cost. If I cannot or will not, you are entitled to a refund of the affected part. Your rights under the Consumer Protection Act remain.

These documents are general information for customers of Metsing.store and do not replace advice from a qualified attorney.
