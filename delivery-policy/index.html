Delivery and Handover Policy

Last updated: 15 November 2026

1. Everything is digital

I do not ship physical goods. I “deliver” by giving you access to, and control of, what I built.

2. Delivery times

ServiceTarget delivery
Ready-made dropshipping store (10 starter products)Within 48 hours (2 business days) of the Start Conditions being met
Launch BundleWithin 48 hours (2 business days) of the Start Conditions being met
Business website (up to 5 pages)Typically within 24 hours (1 business day) of the Start Conditions being met
Add-onsWithin 2 business days of the Start Conditions being met
Company ConsultationDepends on the authority. I give you an estimate. See the Company Consultation Terms.

Business days are Monday to Friday, excluding public holidays.

3. When the timer starts (“Start Conditions”)

The timeline starts when all of these are true:

  1. I have sent the Order Confirmation and you have accepted it (or you paid online after accepting the terms at checkout);
  2. your payment has cleared; and
  3. I have all the information and materials I need (such as your niche choice, business name, contact details, logo or brand preferences, and any accounts or logins needed).

4. What pauses the timeline

The clock pauses while I am waiting for you (missing information, unanswered questions, late approvals, payment problems, or third-party delays such as domain or payment-gateway verification), and during events beyond my control (power or internet outages, hosting or platform outages, and similar events). I will tell you if this affects your delivery date.

5. What counts as “delivered”

Delivery is complete when I have:

  1. built the store, website, logo or add-on as described in the Order Confirmation; and
  2. given you access (for example admin login, store link, or files) and told you in writing (WhatsApp or email) that it is ready.

Delivery is not delayed by things outside the agreed scope, such as your domain taking time to propagate, a payment gateway approving your account, or product details you have not supplied.

6. Handover steps

  1. I send you login details or links and a short hand-over note.
  2. You check the work against the Order Confirmation.
  3. You request revisions (3 rounds within 3 days of delivery) and report any material fault (within 7 days). I fix them.
  4. After you accept, or after 7 days pass without a reported fault, the order is closed. Ongoing help is available through WhatsApp, and the Store Maintenance subscription covers updates and security checks.
  5. I keep a handover record (date, what was delivered, what was confirmed).

7. Late delivery

If I am late for reasons within my control, I will tell you and give you a new date. If I still do not deliver within a reasonable extra time, you can cancel and I will refund the undelivered part. See the Refund Policy.

These documents are general information for customers of Metsing.store and do not replace advice from a qualified attorney.
