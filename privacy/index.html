3. ID documents and sensitive documents

  • I ask for ID copies and proof of address only when a service needs them.
  • I use them only at the time I process your request, and then delete them. I do not keep copies once your task is done.
  • Please send them only through the channel I agree with you (usually WhatsApp or email), never through public social media.
  • I do not share them with anyone except the authority your service needs (for example CIPC, SARS, CSD, CIDB or COIDA).
  • Because documents sent over WhatsApp or email also sit in the chat or mailbox, I delete them from the chat, my phone or computer, and any download folder when I am done. WhatsApp or email providers may keep their own backups, which I cannot control.
  • I will never ask for your banking passwords, PINs or one-time passwords. See the Company Consultation Terms for how I handle government portal access.
  • I keep a record that a service was done (for example the Order Confirmation and the filing reference), but not a copy of your ID document.

5. Sending information outside South Africa

Some providers above (Render, Google and WhatsApp/Meta) process information outside South Africa. I only allow this where the provider is subject to law, binding rules or an agreement that gives substantially similar protection, where it is necessary to carry out our agreement, or where you consent. By using the website and WhatsApp to place an order, you accept these transfers.

6. How I protect information

I take reasonable technical and organisational steps, including HTTPS on the website, limiting access to myself, strong passwords and two-factor authentication on my email, WhatsApp, hosting and payment accounts, keeping devices updated, and deleting documents when I no longer need them. No system is completely secure, but I take reasonable care.

If I believe your information was accessed without authority, I will notify the Information Regulator and you as soon as reasonably possible.

8. Your rights

You can at any time:

  • ask whether I hold information about you and get a copy;
  • ask me to correct inaccurate information;
  • ask me to delete information I no longer need to keep (I may keep what the law requires);
  • object to processing based on legitimate interest, and withdraw consent; and
  • opt out of marketing.

Contact info@metsing.store. I aim to respond within 30 days and may need to confirm your identity.

9. Direct marketing

I only send marketing by WhatsApp, email or SMS if you have opted in, or if you are an existing customer and I am offering similar services and you were given the chance to opt out. Every marketing message lets you opt out.

10. Children

My services are for adults (18 and over). I do not knowingly collect children’s information.
