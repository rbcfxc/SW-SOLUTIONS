Dropshipping is a way to run an online store without keeping stock. When a customer buys from your store, your supplier ships the product directly to them. You only pay for the product after you've made the sale, and you keep the difference as profit.

The Dropshipping Store includes a professional mobile-friendly design, homepage, product pages, cart and checkout, About/Contact/Shipping/Refund pages, 10 starter products and a handover guide. The Business Website includes up to 5 pages, a contact form, WhatsApp button, mobile-friendly design and basic SEO. The Launch Bundle adds a logo and payment gateway setup to the Dropshipping Store.

Most stores and websites are delivered within 48 hours after your order and payment are confirmed. Larger add-ons, like Product Copy & Creatives for many products, may take a little longer. We'll always give you a clear timeline upfront. Company Consultation services follow each authority's own processing times, so those are typical estimates rather than a 48-hour promise.
