Dropshipping is a way to run an online store without keeping stock. When a customer buys from your store, your supplier ships the product directly to them. You only pay for the product after you've made the sale, and you keep the difference as profit.

The Central Supplier Database (CSD) is National Treasury's register of businesses that want to supply goods or services to government. Most national, provincial and municipal departments will only buy from suppliers registered on the CSD, so it's usually a must before you can quote on government work or tenders. Once registered, you get a supplier (MAAA) number and a CSD summary report you can attach to your bids.

The Construction Industry Development Board (CIDB) keeps a register of contractors. If you want to do construction, building, civil, electrical or mechanical work for the public sector, you'll generally need to be CIDB-registered at the right grade for the size of the contract. We'll guide you on which class of work and grade you're likely to qualify for, and help you prepare and submit your application.

COIDA (the Compensation for Occupational Injuries and Diseases Act) requires employers to register with the Compensation Fund so workers are covered if they're injured or fall ill because of their work. Clients and government departments often ask contractors for a valid Letter of Good Standing as proof that you're registered and up to date. Without it, you may not be allowed on site or be able to get paid. We register you and help you apply for the letter.

Government/statutory fees are separate from our service fees unless specifically stated. Payments to CIPC, CIDB, SARS and the COIDA/Compensation Fund must be made directly to them. We'll always let you know the exact statutory amount payable before you pay the authority.

Beyond ready-made online stores and websites, our Company Consultation covers company registration, CSD, COIDA, CIDB, CIPC changes, SARS compliance and business support documents—and you're welcome to ask us on WhatsApp about anything not listed.
