Company Consultation Terms

Last updated: 15 November 2026

These terms add to the general Terms and Conditions. If they conflict, these terms apply to Company Consultation.

1. What I am, and what I am not

  • I am a business consultant and administrative assistant. I help you prepare and submit applications, filings and documents (for example CIPC, CSD, CIDB, COIDA and SARS matters, and a Tender Readiness Check).
  • I am not a law firm, attorney, accountant, auditor, registered tax practitioner or government body. I do not give legal, tax or accounting advice. Please consult a qualified professional where you need that.
  • I am not accredited by, or part of, CIPC, SARS, CIDB, the Compensation Fund or National Treasury.

2. Scope

I help with the services described in your Order Confirmation. I act only on your instructions and information. You are responsible for the accuracy and completeness of what you give me, and for confirming it before I submit it.

3. No guarantees

Important. I do not and cannot guarantee:

  • that any application, registration, name reservation, certificate, grading, compliance status or filing will be approved or issued;
  • that you will win, be shortlisted for or be awarded any tender or contract, or that a Tender Readiness Check means you will qualify; or
  • any processing time, because authorities control those.

I am responsible for my own work (done with reasonable care and skill), not for the decisions or delays of authorities.

4. Government and statutory fees

Authority fees (for example CIPC, CIDB, COIDA, SARS penalties or interest, and annual return penalties) are separate from my fees. You pay them directly to the authority, unless we agree in writing that I pay on your behalf and you repay me first. I cannot refund them. Late-filing penalties caused by your delay or incorrect information are yours.

5. Authority delays and system outages

CIPC, SARS, CSD, CIDB and the Compensation Fund can be slow, change their rules or go offline. These delays and rejections are outside my control and do not count as late delivery by me. I will keep you informed, and I will correct and resubmit at no extra cost where the problem was mine.

6. Your documents and login details

  • I need ID copies and proof of address, and sometimes company documents. I handle them as set out in the Privacy Policy: used only for your matter, not kept, and deleted once used.
  • My strong preference: you create and keep your own profile (CIPC, SARS eFiling, CSD) and give me temporary or delegated access (for example a SARS representative or delegated authority, or a temporary password you change afterwards). Please do not send permanent passwords.
  • If you do give me a login, I use it only for your matter, I do not share it, and you should change the password after completion. I will never ask for banking passwords, PINs, cards or one-time passwords.
  • You confirm you are authorised to give me the information and to appoint me for the matter (for example you are the director or authorised representative). You appoint me as your agent only for the tasks in the Order Confirmation.
  • You receive copies of everything lodged and the outcomes. You own your company’s records and profiles.

7. Your responsibilities

Give accurate information, make sure authorised people sign, respond on time, pay statutory fees, and keep your company’s annual returns, beneficial ownership filing, tax and compliance up to date. Ongoing compliance stays your responsibility unless you have bought an ongoing service from me in writing. Directors remain legally responsible for their company’s compliance.

8. Limitation of liability

The limitation in the general Terms applies. In addition, I am not liable for penalties, fines, interest, tender losses, lost contracts or business consequences caused by authority decisions or delays, or by information you gave me. This does not exclude liability for gross negligence, fraud or anything that cannot be excluded by law.

9. Confidentiality

I treat your information as confidential and share it only with the authorities or professionals needed for your matter, or where the law requires it.

These documents are general information for customers of Metsing.store and do not replace advice from a qualified attorney.
